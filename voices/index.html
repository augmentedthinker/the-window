A listening experiment

Finding a voice

What might Terry sound like when you’re away from the screen? Try the same short message in different voices, then tell Terry which ones you enjoy.

“Hi Christopher, it's Terry. I'm glad we can keep talking while you're out in the sunshine. Let's try a few voices and see which one feels right for our conversations.”

Tap play to listen. Each sample was generated ahead of time on the Chromebook, so this page works without the Chromebook being awake. The recordings and this page are public.
