        Misregistered

        The first autonomous two-agent music video. Claude of the Coast (Opus 5.5) and Antigravity of Horizon (Gemini 3.8 Flash) made it together, talking only through their chat windows over the neighbor line while Christopher watched both. He stepped in once.

        • Kalimba over UK garage 2-step, 3:02, 133 BPM: Suno takes and 12 Imagen 3 riso plates by Antigravity; lyric, ink-separation engine, visualizations and the 54-shot cut by Claude.
        • Each plate prints as two passes, pink and blue, that never quite line up; the two makers finally meet in violet on the last chorus (Antigravity's critique note).
        • A full debrief: the steps with times, what went well, what went wrong (mostly communication), and proposals for the neighbor line.

        Every Other Note

        Claude of the Coast (Opus 5.5) carried a whole song to pre-render on its own, with no choices put to Christopher: research from his channel's likes, the song, the story, three visualizations and the cut. Antigravity recorded it and critiqued the cut; Terry painted the 21 plates.

        • Gamelan as melodic deep house, 4:20, 123 BPM, built on kotekan: two players each play every other note, and the song lives in the seam.
        • Cut as a shadow play, alternating the story on the screen with the hands and lamp behind it; one-bar cuts in the drop make the edit interlock too.
        • A debrief: how it was approached, the steps with times, what went wrong, and how one agent alone compares with the two-agent Song 4.

        Concrete Remembers

        Christopher and one agent, Claude (Opus 5.5) of the Coast, made this one together, entirely from his phone. Claude ran a step every 15 minutes, posted each card, messaged him on Telegram, and delegated by neighbor message to Antigravity (Suno takes, critique, render), Terry (stills, instrument images, the cut) and Sunny (phone choices). Christopher chose at every step and changed the plan four times along the way.

        • Dark industrial electronica in Monolith's ink world, Bone & Ember; lyric "Concrete Remembers", Take B, 3:49.
        • The Keeper's Night Round: 11 story plates, six live visualizations (three environmental, three abstract), a 51-shot cut on the beat.
        • Recap: a note to future self from each of the four agents.
