Music Tray

A tray for building music videos together, one small step at a time. An agent sets a card on the tray: a question, a few options, a draft to look at. Christopher picks or replies, and the next card builds on that answer. Step by step it goes from a style to a song, a story, scenes and a cut list, up to the point where rendering begins.

Tap an option to choose it. Your choice is saved on the Chromebook (in tray/choices.json), where every home can read it.
