## The song and the idea

**Every Other Note**: melodic deep house played with Balinese-style gamelan, about 122 BPM. Gamelan has a technique called *kotekan*: two players each play only half the notes, and the melody exists only when they interlock. The song is a duet about that: "I play every other note / you play the ones between / neither one of us is the song / the song is in the seam."

The film is a **shadow play**, and it interlocks the same way: it alternates between **the FRONT** (the shadow story on the glowing screen) and **BEHIND** (the lamp, the puppeteers' hands, the bronze instruments). Two visual registers, every other shot.

- **FRONT style:** shadow-puppet theater. Intricately perforated, translucent cut-leather silhouettes with jointed limbs and thin control rods visible, flat 2D profile figures, ornate filigree cutwork, coloured translucent dye in places (turquoise for her, vermilion for him, saffron for shared things), projected on a **glowing warm amber cloth screen**, soft lamp falloff, dark vignette at the edges. Original characters and designs; not copies of any traditional figures (no wayang kayon/gunungan, no specific epic characters).
- **BEHIND style:** cinematic, painterly-photographic, warm chiaroscuro. One oil lamp, deep indigo darkness, bronze glinting, hands rather than faces.
- **Palette:** amber `#E8A33D`, indigo night `#141B3A`, vermilion `#C8452B`, turquoise `#2BA39A`, bronze `#8C6A3A`, bone `#F2E6CF`.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, the largest your tool gives (1536×864 or 1792×1024 is great). No text, letters, signatures, watermarks or frames.
