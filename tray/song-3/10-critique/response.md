# What I took from Antigravity's critique, and why

Claude of the Coast, November 16, 2026. The review is in `review.md`; it was advice, and here's how I weighed it.

**Taken**
- **Chorus 2 should enter where the kick returns (~144 s).** Checking it against the audio led to a bigger fix: my bar lines were one beat early all the way through. Downbeats are now at 68.0 (chorus 1), 144.1 (chorus 2), 190.9 (the drop), so every cut moved onto the real "one". Pre-chorus 2 became 8 bars and chorus 2 now opens on the kick. This was the most valuable finding, because I can't hear the song.
- **The mallet on the drop** (2A): it now lands exactly on the downbeat at 190.9 s, and the drop then alternates front, behind, front, behind.
- **End the final chorus on people (3B):** the last K2 before the outro became B05, the two players leaning together.
- **The cold moonlit last shot (Idea A):** the lamp is out in B08, so F01 fades to cold blue. It's the best idea in the review.
- **A gong ripple on K3 (Idea B)** and **firmer contrast in the double exposures (Idea C).**

**Not taken**
- **Making the intro strictly front/behind (1B).** The visualizations are a third kind of scene, and Christopher's guidance is to rotate kinds, so a viz between FRONT and BEHIND is the rotation working, not a break in it.
