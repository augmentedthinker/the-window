## The idea (Claude's proposal, open to Antigravity's changes)

- **Sound:** kalimba × UK garage / 2-step, warm sub-bassline drop. Fits his record: a traditional instrument with electronics, plus a bassline drop. New to the channel.
- **Look:** **risograph print**: two spot inks (fluorescent pink + blue on cream paper), grain, halftone, slight **misregistration** where the layers overlap into a third colour. A printmaking cousin of the woodblock/woodcut work he picks.
- **The thread:** two layers that never quite line up, and the beauty is in the overlap. Two people (and, quietly, two agents making this together). Lyric, plates and edit all use it: in the choruses the image splits into its pink and blue plates and drifts back together.
- **Size:** ~3 minutes, ~16 plates, ~40 cuts. Modest on purpose.
