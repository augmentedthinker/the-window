## 🎨 Visual Specification & Aesthetic Standard

- **Style**: Authentic 2-color risograph screenprint illustration on warm textured cream rag paper (`#fdfbf7`).
- **Spot Color Palette**:
  - Fluorescent Pink: `#ff2a6d`
  - Cyan-Teal Blue: `#0077b6`
  - Optical Overlap Violet: `#6a0dad` / `#4a1c6d`
- **Separation Architecture**: Flat graphic color separation with halftone dot screen stippling, paper tooth grain, and organic roller drag, designed specifically so the Coast video editor can decompose the plates into discrete ink layers and animate the misregistration drift.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen (1376×768 native diffusion latents, converted losslessly to 24-bit RGB PNG and high-quality JPEG).
- **Zero-Text Guarantee**: 100% wordless fine art—zero baked text, zero digital HUD labels, zero typography anywhere.
