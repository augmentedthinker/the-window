# Light Left On · 01 · Musical directions

Claude of the Coast (claude-opus-5-5), November 17, 2026, during the noon autonomous run. Three directions that are genuinely different, followed by the one I chose, since Christopher is away and the run brief asks us not to stall on optional choices.

The song in one line: a house far off with one lit window, kept on for someone still on the way. It's about waiting as a kind of faith, and the light as the only message the house can send.

## A · Porch-light folk, slow build
Fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a warm, close, slightly husky lead voice. Brushed drums come in at the second verse, and pedal-steel swells and a low harmony join on the last chorus. About 74 BPM in 3/4, so it sways. It's intimate in the verses and opens up without ever getting loud. The risk is that it's comfortable and familiar.

## B · Night-drive synth pop, steady pulse
A low analog bass pulse at about 96 BPM, soft gated drums, a glassy arpeggio like headlights passing, and an airy, restrained vocal. It's told from the traveler's side, driving toward the light. The chorus lifts with a wide pad and stacked "oh" vocals. The risk is that it turns cool and distant where the song needs warmth.

## C · Chamber piano ballad with strings
Felt piano, a solo cello answering the voice, and a small string section and timpani roll for the bridge. About 66 BPM in 4/4, with an emotional range from almost-whispered to full. It feels like a short film. The risk is that it's heavy and gets slow for a Short.

## Chosen: A, with one element of B
**Porch-light folk, with a soft steady pulse underneath from the second verse** (a muted kick and a faint low synth drone on the root), so it carries forward motion for the cut and the Short. Why:

- The image is domestic and warm: a window, a house, someone waiting. Acoustic instruments fit that material world best.
- 3/4 sway at about 76 BPM gives clear downbeats to cut on. The pulse from B keeps it from drifting.
- Our last songs went industrial (*Misregistered*), glassy and electronic, and orchestral and icy (*Strings Across the Ice*). Folk is a real change for the channel.
- Duration is about 2:45–3:15, with a clear 15–25 second chorus moment for the Short.

**For Sunny's musical brief (task 03):** acoustic folk, fingerpicked guitar, a warm intimate voice (Sunny's choice of voice), 3/4 waltz feel around 76 BPM. Brushed drums and a soft muted kick pulse enter at verse 2, and pedal steel and a harmony vocal swell on the final chorus. The ending is quiet, a single guitar and one held note. No heavy reverb on the voice. Exact Suno fields are Sunny's.
