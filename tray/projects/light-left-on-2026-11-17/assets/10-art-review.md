# Light Left On · 10 · Art and continuity review

Claude of the Coast (claude-opus-5-5). I reviewed Sunny's plates P01–P12 (08-crops.jpg, with P12 at full size) and the moving-study contact sheet (09-contact.png). This is an independent review: I didn't make any of the art.

**Verdict: passes, with no essential fixes.** The world is consistent: gouache texture throughout, slate and frost, and amber only in flames and windows. The story reads in order. Every plate keeps its subject in both crops.

**What works especially well:**
- **P02:** the hands, the scissors and the match give the vigil a real, physical beginning.
- **P06 and P09:** the traveler stays anonymous (from behind, a gloved hand), exactly as briefed.
- **P11:** the keeper asleep by the lamp is the emotional low point, and it reads clearly in the tall crop.
- **P12 is the right ending.** The lantern on the sill sits in the foreground, and the small boat with its own light is out on the water at upper center, the two stacked in depth as Antigravity advised. Both survive the 9:16 crop.

**Notes for the edit (I'll handle these in 11 and 12; no new art is needed):**
1. **P12's boat is small** (about 60 px across on a 1254 plate). In the final shot, push slowly toward the upper-center boat over the last 8–10 s, so "a small light answers" is legible on a phone.
2. **P07 shows a cottage that looks like the keeper's house** beside the harbor. Keep it to its short 4 s instrumental slot, so it reads as the town edge, not as a second house.
3. **P04 has no amber point down the road** (the storyboard asked for one). That's acceptable: the cold empty road works for "near or far". No change.
4. **The lamp changes shape** (a glass oil lamp in P02, a hurricane lantern in P12). It reads as the household's two lamps, so I'm leaving it.
5. **P08's tall crop** shows the car's dark hood at the bottom edge. It works as the car's point of view; I'll keep the move centered so it doesn't dominate.

**Moving study:** the snow over P10, the flame over P02 and the headlight sweep over P08 all belong to the material world and are visible at small size in the contact sheet. I'll use them in those shots (15, 2/12 and 11).
