[Intro – fingerpicked guitar]

[Verse 1]
The kettle's cold, the clock is slow,
the road's a ribbon in the snow.
I wiped the glass, I trimmed the wick,
the dark comes early and comes thick.
I don't know if you're near or far,
I only know just where you are:
somewhere out past the last of the trees,
finding your way through the winter trees.

[Chorus]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.

[Verse 2]
The ferry's late, the wires are down,
there's ice along the edge of town.
I set two cups, pull out your chair
the way it was when you were there.
And every car that slows its wheels,
I tell my heart, be still, be still.
It isn't much that I can send,
a yellow square at the world's end.

[Chorus]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.

[Bridge – softer, then building]
And if you don't come home tonight,
I'll leave it on till morning light.
And if you don't come home this year,
the glass will know that I was here.

[Final Chorus – full, harmony]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.
Look for the light left on.

[Outro – single guitar, one held note]
Far out on the water,
a small light answers.
