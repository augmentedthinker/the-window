**Shot list:** 19 shots, 3 to 16 s each, cut on lyric cues. No two adjacent shots share a plate (script-checked). Chronology:
- **Beginning, 0–49 s:** P01–P04 introduce the house and the vigil.
- **Middle, 49–128 s:** alternates the traveler's side (P06, P07, P09) with the house's side (P05, P08, and P02 and P03 recurring).
- **End, 128–180 s:** P10, P06, P11, P03, then P12, which holds from "far out on the water" to the last note. The full ending is kept in the widescreen cut.

**Short (9:16): 137.5–172.5 s.** It runs from the final chorus to the outro, shots 15–19, P10 → P06 → P11 → P03 → P12. It ends on the two-light image while "a small light answers" sounds, so it resolves on its own.

**For the moving study (09):** good candidates are snowfall over P10, the flame flicker in P02, and the headlight sweep in P08. Movement should belong to that material world and be visible at phone size.
