# Light Left On · 02 · Lyrics and story seed

Claude of the Coast (claude-opus-5-5), November 17, 2026. Written for direction A (porch-light folk in 3/4, about 76 BPM, with a soft pulse from verse 2). The section tags are for Suno. Sunny may trim lines to fit the brief.

## Lyrics

[Intro – fingerpicked guitar]

[Verse 1]
The kettle's cold, the clock is slow,
the road's a ribbon in the snow.
I wiped the glass, I trimmed the wick,
the dark comes early and comes thick.
I don't know if you're near or far,
I only know just where you are:
somewhere out past the last of the trees,
coming home on your hands and knees.

[Chorus]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.

[Verse 2]
The ferry's late, the wires are down,
there's ice along the edge of town.
I set two cups, I set one chair
the way it was when you were there.
And every car that slows its wheels,
I tell my heart, be still, be still.
It isn't much that I can send,
a yellow square at the world's end.

[Chorus]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.

[Bridge – softer, then building]
And if you don't come home tonight,
I'll leave it on till morning light.
And if you don't come home this year,
the glass will know that I was here.

[Final Chorus – full, harmony]
So I leave the light left on,
one small window, burning long.
If you lose the road, if the night is wrong,
look for the light left on.
Look for the light left on.

[Outro – single guitar, one held note]
Far out on the water,
a small light answers.

## Story seed (beginning, middle, end)

A house on a low shore at the edge of a small town in winter. Someone (the Keeper; their face is barely seen, so they could be anyone) keeps one window lit for a person traveling home across water and snow. We never see the reunion. The answer is a second light.

**Beginning: the vigil is set** (intro, verse 1)
1. Wide dusk: the house far off across a snowy shore, one window lit. This is the title image.
2. Close: hands trimming an oil lamp's wick. A match, then the flame.
3. Frosted window seen from outside, a warm yellow square in the blue.
4. The road out of town under snow, empty, with a line of bare trees.

**Middle: waiting, and the traveler** (chorus, verse 2, chorus)
5. Kitchen table: two cups, one chair pulled out.
6. Traveler (seen from behind, in a coat and hood) on a small ferry deck at night, sleet, looking ahead.
7. Ice along the harbor edge, town lights, downed wires.
8. Headlights slowing on the road, then passing. The window again, unchanged.
9. Traveler's hand on the cold rail. Far ahead, a tiny yellow point.

**End: the light holds, the answer** (bridge, final chorus, outro)
10. Night deepens, snow heavier. The window is still lit (time passing: the clock, the lamp lower).
11. Pre-dawn blue: the Keeper asleep in the chair by the window. The lamp is still burning.
12. From the window: far out on the dark water, a small lantern light answers.
13. Last image: both lights in one frame, the window and the lantern on the water, at first light.

**Continuity notes for visuals (Sunny) and the cut (me):**
- Recurring anchors: the yellow window, the oil lamp, the traveler's dark coat and hood, the ferry's lantern.
- Palette: winter blue-gray with one warm amber (window, lamp, lantern). Amber appears only in the light sources.
- Keep each frame square, with the light source or subject near the center, so the 16:9 and 9:16 crops both hold it.
- No two adjacent cuts from the same scene. Alternate house-side and traveler-side shots in the middle section.
